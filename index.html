📦 Case Study 1

Last-Mile Coordination & Delay Mitigation

The Operational Challenge

A cross-scheduling error threatened to paralyze the delivery of critical goods (sugar bales, rice quintals, and oil boxes) valued at $5,725.00. The original plan collapsed the expected delivery times for a multi-destination corridor (Guayaquil → Chongón → Playas → Salinas), jeopardizing customer satisfaction and requiring immediate, self-directed action.

🎯 Intervention & Execution

Vendor and Documentation Management:
Autonomously coordinated an emergency solution, redirecting the cargo to an external logistics provider to segment the operation into simultaneous routes.

Cost Optimization & Control:
Negotiated directly with the provider to reduce the contingency rate from $500 to $350, protecting the operation's budget without compromising established timelines.

Quality Assurance & Communication:
Faced with the vendor's requirement to use their own delivery personnel, I structured a plan incorporating an internal stevedore to ensure a seamless transition and maintain total end-customer trust.

✨ Measurable Impact & Results

  • Service Level (OTD): 100% on-time delivery before 7:00 PM on the same day
  • Budget Efficiency: 30% savings on unforeseen freight costs ($150 reduction)
  • Risk Mitigation: Zero returns or penalties on the $5,725.00 billed
  • Operational: Maintained customer confidence across 4-destination route

📊 Case Study 2

Data Audit, Systems & Traceability Control

The Operational Challenge

In an environment requiring strict administrative control, a massive audit of physical and electronic inventories was necessary across three Temporary Customs Warehouses (Intercarga, Cargoservice, and TCE Consumo). The process involved detecting data discrepancies between current platforms (ECUAPASS) and obsolete records from the previous system (SICE), creating a documentation bottleneck and database inconsistencies.

🎯 Intervention & Execution

Systems & Data Management:
Conducted the review, cross-referencing, and updating of massive data sets across multiple systems and official communication channels (Quipux and emails).

Issue Tracking & Escalation:
Executed rigorous control with up to three rounds of formal requests to demand answers and escalate missing information or incomplete files to the warehouses.

Detailed Documentary Research:
In the absence of digital traceability in older archives, I independently led a manual search through physical paper files to match and correct historical records.

✨ Measurable Impact & Results

  • Volume of Data Managed: 600–900 total MRN records processed (200–300 per warehouse)
  • Time Efficiency: Completed entire audit within strict 3-week timeline
  • Discrepancies Found: 20 critical non-compliance discrepancies identified across 600+ records
  • Regulatory Compliance: Corrective actions implemented; warehouse sanctions assessed
  • Concurrent Operations: Completed while maintaining daily administrative duties
